Watamu Blue Safari

Set out by boat for a full-day Watamu Blue Safari. Look out for dolphins when they are in season (sightings are not guaranteed), snorkel in Watamu Marine National Park, and take a boat ride through the mangroves. Pause on a sandbank or island for a fresh seafood lunch before returning along the coast.
