Booking terms and conditions

  • All listed departures are open for booking. Trips proceed when at least 6 travellers book, with a maximum of 15 people.

  • International flights are not included. Your selected Friday is arrival and group meet-up day at City Blue Creekside Hotel in Mombasa.
  • Enter passport details exactly as shown on your passport. Ask Shujaa to confirm any other terms before paying.
