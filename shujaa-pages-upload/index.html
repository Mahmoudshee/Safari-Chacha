Kenya · Small group safari & coast

Tsavo Safari
& Watamu Coast

A nine-day coast-and-safari journey from Mombasa to Tsavo East and Watamu.

★★★★★  Designed and hosted by Shujaa Tours And Adventures
Duration9 days · 8 nights
Group size6–15 travellers
Travel styleSafari & soft adventure
Start / finishMombasa, Kenya
Arrive curious. Leave with stories you’ll tell for years.

Begin in Mombasa, head inland for guided wildlife viewing in Tsavo East, then return to the coast. Discover the Marafa canyon, enjoy Watamu beaches and islands, and share the journey with experienced local guides.

The good stuff, taken care of

What’s included

Thoughtfully planned, from first hello to last game drive.
✳Tsavo East safari from Days 2–5, with a professional guide and private jeep
✳Three nights at a 3- or 4-star savannah resort, full board, overlooking a wildlife watering spring
✳Four nights in Watamu at a private villa or 4-star hotel/resort with a pool
✳One night in a beachfront Mombasa hotel with a pool
✳Private jeep, professional guide and driver from Days 2–6
✳Park fees and tickets, including Marafa Hell’s Kitchen entrance
✳Full board on Tsavo East safari stay days, plus the included savannah aperitif and Marafa sunset aperitif
✳Full-time coordinator assistance; free trip changes up to 31 days before departure

Not included

International return flights, meals and drinks where not specifically listed, extra drinks, the optional night safari, optional activities and personal purchases. A shared activity fund of approximately €150 is planned for group-chosen extras; confirm the contribution details with the coordinator.

Two group departures each month

Choose a departure date

Two Friday departures each month

Choose one of the two scheduled Friday departures: the first Friday in days 1–9 or the last Friday in the final 9 days of the month. Late-month trips may finish in the following month.

Prices are per person and follow the current EUR exchange rate. July, August, November and December are high season, with prices up to €2,700; other months start from €2,400. Every Friday is the arrival and group meet-up day at City Blue Creekside Hotel, Mombasa. All listed dates are open for booking. Trips proceed with at least 6 travellers and a maximum of 15; select any date to continue.
