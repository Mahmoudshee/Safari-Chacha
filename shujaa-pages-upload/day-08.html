Sardegna Due Island

Take time to relax after the excursions. Spend the day around Sardegna Due Island in Watamu, with the option to snorkel or go diving. The itinerary describes a seafood barbecue and Swahili food on the island; confirm with the coordinator which meals or activities are included in your package. In the evening, visit LichtHaus at Mida Creek and enjoy the waterside atmosphere.
